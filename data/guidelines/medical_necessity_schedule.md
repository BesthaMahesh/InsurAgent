# MEDICAL NECESSITY AND ITEM ADMISSIBILITY SCHEDULE (SAMPLE DEMO)
# DOCUMENT CODE: GUIDE-MED-ADMISSIBILITY-2026

## 1. Non-Payable and Consumable Items Schedule
- Non-medical expenses: Administrative admission fees, registration charges, medical record documentation charges, phone/Wi-Fi charges.
- Personal comfort items: Diapers, wipes, sanitizers beyond clinical requirement, thermometer charges for personal use.
- Disposable surgical kits: Reimbursable up to capped benchmark of INR 2,500 per surgery unless specialty implants specified.

## 2. Standard Length of Stay Benchmarks
- Normal Spontaneous Vaginal Delivery: 48 hours maximum admissible stay.
- Caesarean Section (C-Section): 72 hours maximum admissible stay.
- Laparoscopic Appendectomy / Cholecystectomy: 48 to 72 hours stay.
- Inguinal Hernia Repair: 24 to 48 hours stay.
- Angioplasty with Single Stent: 48 to 72 hours stay.
