# CLAIMS ASSESSMENT AND ADJUDICATION GUIDELINES (SAMPLE DEMO)
# DOCUMENT CODE: GUIDE-CLM-2026-v1

## 1. Mandatory Documentation Checklist
- Health Claims: Discharge Summary, Original Itemized Hospital Bills, Pharmacy Bills with Prescriptions, Diagnostic Reports.
- Motor Claims: Duly filled claim form, Driver's License copy, Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), Repair Estimate from workshop, Spot photos.
- Travel Claims: Airline tickets, Boarding passes, PIR for baggage claims, Original medical reports.

## 2. Evidence Grounding & Adjudication Rules
- An agent or adjudicator must verify that the hospital or garage is recognized and bills are itemized.
- Incurred amounts exceeding the sanctioned limits must be adjusted according to policy deductibles and co-pays.
- Any mismatch between claimed invoice amount and verified breakdown must be flagged for human review.
