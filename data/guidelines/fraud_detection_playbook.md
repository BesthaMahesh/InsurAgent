## 1. High-Risk Indicators Across Insurance Lines
1.1 Health Claims Red Flags:
- Claim incurred within 30 days of initial policy inception or immediate renewal reinstatement.
- Repeated claims from non-accredited or blacklisted clinical nursing homes with handwritten un-itemized invoices.
- Incongruence between reported surgical procedure codes and standard length of hospital stay.

1.2 Motor Claims Red Flags:
- High-damage single-vehicle collision occurring during late-night hours without third-party witnesses or police diary entry.
- Vehicle repair estimate exceeding 60% of Insured Declared Value (IDV) filed within 60 days of purchasing comprehensive add-on covers.
- Discrepancy between vehicle damage photos (paint transfer, impact direction) and claimant's statement of loss.

1.3 Travel Claims Red Flags:
- Lost baggage claims without airline Property Irregularity Report (PIR) stamped within 24 hours of flight arrival.
- High-value electronics purchase receipts dated after the alleged date of theft.
