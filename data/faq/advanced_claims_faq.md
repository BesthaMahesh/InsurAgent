# ADVANCED CLAIMS AND POLICY FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (SAMPLE DEMO)

Q1: What is No Claim Bonus (NCB) and how does a claim impact it under motor insurance?
A1: Under POL-MOTOR-COMP-2026, No Claim Bonus is a progressive discount on own damage premium earned for claim-free policy years. Filing any own damage claim during the policy tenure resets the NCB tier to 0% upon next renewal unless an NCB Protector add-on is active.

Q2: What is the difference between Cashless and Reimbursement claim settlement?
A2: In cashless claims, the insurer settles approved eligible expenses directly with the network hospital or authorized workshop. In reimbursement claims, the claimant settles bills out-of-pocket and submits original receipts and discharge documents within 30 days for post-adjudication reimbursement.

Q3: Does the Commercial Property Policy cover business interruption without physical damage?
A3: No, under POL-PROPERTY-BIZ-2026 Section 1.2, business interruption coverage is contingent upon direct physical destruction or damage caused by an insured peril (e.g. fire, flood, cyclone).
