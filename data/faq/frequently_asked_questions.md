# INSURANCE CLAIMS FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (SAMPLE DEMO)

Q1: What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases under the Gold Health Policy?
A1: Under Section 2.1 of POL-HEALTH-GOLD-2026, the Pre-Existing Disease (PED) waiting period is 24 continuous months from policy inception.

Q2: Are day-care treatments covered under health insurance?
A2: Yes, day-care procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization (e.g., cataract, chemotherapy, dialysis) are covered under Section 1.2.

Q3: What is the deductible on motor vehicle claims?
A3: Deductibles vary by vehicle type, with standard depreciation applied to plastic, glass, and metal parts per Section 2 of POL-MOTOR-COMP-2026.
