# DEMO TRAVEL SHIELD POLICY (SAMPLE DATASET)
# POLICY CODE: POL-TRAVEL-SHIELD-2026
# NOTICE: This is sample demonstration data for testing InsurAgent. Not an official insurance contract.

## Section 1: Trip Cancellation & Interruption
- Reimburses non-refundable trip costs up to USD 5,000 / INR 4,00,000 due to unforeseen illness, injury, or severe weather.
- Written medical certificate certifying inability to travel is mandatory.

## Section 2: Emergency Medical & Evacuation
- Emergency hospitalization abroad up to USD 100,000.
- Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation of mortal remains.
- Deductible: USD 100 per claim.

## Section 3: Baggage Loss and Flight Delay
- Checked baggage delay exceeding 6 continuous hours: Allowance of USD 200 for essential purchases.
- Total loss of checked-in baggage: Reimbursed up to USD 1,000 with airline Property Irregularity Report (PIR).
- Flight delay over 4 hours: Meals and accommodation allowance up to USD 300.
