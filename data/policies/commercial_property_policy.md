## Section 1: Standard Fire and Special Perils (SFSP)
1.1 Covered Risks:
- Direct physical destruction or damage to commercial premises, machinery, stock-in-trade caused by fire, lightning, explosion/implosion.
- Storm, cyclone, typhoon, tempest, hurricane, tornado, flood, and inundation (STFI perils).
- Impact damage by any rail/road vehicle or animal by direct contact.
- Subsidence and landslide including rockslide.

1.2 Business Interruption (Consequential Loss):
- Indemnifies actual loss of gross profit resulting from interruption of business operations up to 12 consecutive months (Indemnity Period).
- Covers standing charges (salaries, lease rent, loan interest) during repairs.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Excess
2.1 Exclusions:
- Spontaneous combustion, fermentation, or heating process without actual fire.
- Nuclear radiation, radioactive contamination, or war risk.
- Theft during or after the occurrence of any insured peril unless force and violence is proved.
- Losses resulting from total shutdown exceeding 30 continuous days without prior notice.

2.2 Excess / Deductible:
- Material Damage: 5% of claim amount subject to a minimum of INR 25,000 for each and every claim.
- Business Interruption: 7 days of Gross Profit excess.
