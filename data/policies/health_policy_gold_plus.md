# DEMO HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY: GOLD PLUS (SAMPLE DATASET)
# POLICY CODE: POL-HEALTH-GOLD-2026
# NOTICE: This is sample demonstration data for testing InsurAgent. Not an official insurance contract.

## Section 1: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage
1.1 Room Rent & ICU Limits:
- Normal Room Rent: 1% of the Sum Insured per day.
- Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges: 2% of the Sum Insured per day.
- Proportionate deductions apply to associate medical expenses if room rent exceeds the entitlement.

1.2 Covered Treatments & Procedures:
- Inpatient hospitalization requiring a minimum continuous stay of 24 hours is covered.
- Day care procedures (e.g., Cataract surgery, Dialysis, Chemotherapy) are covered without 24-hour stay requirement.
- Emergency room treatment resulting in immediate admission is eligible.

1.3 Pre and Post-Hospitalization:
- Pre-hospitalization medical expenses incurred up to 30 days prior to admission are covered.
- Post-hospitalization medical expenses incurred up to 60 days post-discharge are covered.

## Section 2: Standard Exclusions & Waiting Periods
2.1 Waiting Periods:
- Initial 30-day waiting period from policy inception for all illnesses (except accidental injury).
- Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) waiting period: 24 months of continuous coverage.
- Specific ailments (Hernia, Cataract, Joint replacements) waiting period: 12 months.

2.2 Exclusions:
- Cosmetic, aesthetic, or obesity treatments are strictly excluded.
- Self-inflicted injuries and hazardous adventure sports injuries are excluded.
- Domiciliary hospitalization without prior insurer pre-authorization is non-payable.
- Non-allopathic treatments without NABH accredited institution certificate are not eligible.

## Section 3: Deductibles and Co-payments
- A standard deductible of INR 5,000 per claim applies.
- Co-payment of 10% applies if treatment is taken in a non-network hospital outside Tier-1 cities.
