## Section 1: Own Damage (OD) Coverage
1.1 Accidental Damage:
- Loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental external means, collision, overturning.
- Natural calamities including flood, typhoon, earthquake, cyclone, and inundation.
- Riot, strike, malicious act, and terrorist activities.

1.2 Theft and Total Loss:
- Constructive Total Loss (CTL) is recognized if aggregate repair estimate exceeds 75% of the Insured Declared Value (IDV).
- FIR copy and non-traceable certificate mandatory for theft claims.

## Section 2: Depreciation on Parts
- Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, tyres, tubes, batteries: 50% depreciation.
- Fibre glass components: 30% depreciation.
- Glass parts: Nil (0%) depreciation.
- Metal parts: Slabs from 0% (up to 6 months) to 50% (over 10 years).

## Section 3: Exclusions
- Driving under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs.
- Driving without a valid driving licence at the time of the accident.
- Consequential loss, depreciation, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown.
- Commercial usage of private vehicles without passenger transport endorsement.
- Claims without valid photographic proof of vehicular damage at the scene or authorized garage.
