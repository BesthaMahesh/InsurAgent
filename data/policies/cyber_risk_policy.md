# DEMO ENTERPRISE CYBER RISK & DATA BREACH POLICY (SAMPLE DATASET)
# POLICY CODE: POL-CYBER-SHIELD-2026
# NOTICE: Sample demonstration policy wording for InsurAgent AI evaluation.

## Section 1: First-Party Incident Response Coverage
1.1 Forensic & Legal Response:
- IT forensic investigation costs to determine source and scope of cyber breach (up to INR 25,00,000).
- Legal counsel expenses for regulatory notifications and mandatory compliance reporting.

1.2 Business Interruption & Data Restoration:
- Lost business income incurred during IT system downtime directly caused by an unauthorized cyber extortion event or malware attack.
- System restoration and data reconstruction expenses from validated offline backups.

1.3 Ransomware / Cyber Extortion:
- Negotiation, advisory, and extortion settlement reimbursement with mandatory pre-approval from the Insurer's authorized cyber incident response panel.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Conditions
2.1 Exclusions:
- Unpatched known critical vulnerabilities with public exploits older than 90 days.
- Infrastructure outages caused by core public telecommunication failure or cloud service provider tier-1 power outage.
- Losses arising from intentional insider sabotage by C-suite officers without criminal complaint filing.

2.2 Waiting Period & Retention:
- 12 hours time retention for Business Interruption claims.
- Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) warranty across all administrative access portals.
