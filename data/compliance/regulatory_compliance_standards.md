# REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT STANDARDS (SAMPLE DEMO)
# DOCUMENT CODE: COMP-IRDA-REG-2026

## 1. Traceability and Explainability Mandate
- Every claim decision, whether automated recommendation or manual settlement, must produce an immutable audit record.
- All policy clauses and knowledge sources used in evaluating coverage must be explicitly cited in the assessment report.
- The reason for claim adjustment, reduction, or rejection must be clearly communicated to the policyholder in plain language.

## 2. Fair Treatment & Non-Discrimination
- Claims evaluation algorithms must not use non-admissible demographic indicators to bias risk scores.
- High-risk or contested claims must be mandatorily assigned to an authorized Human Adjuster before final settlement.
