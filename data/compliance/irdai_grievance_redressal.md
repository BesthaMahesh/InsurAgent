# IRDAI CLAIMS TURNAROUND TIME & GRIEVANCE STANDARDS (SAMPLE DEMO)
# DOCUMENT CODE: COMP-IRDAI-TAT-2026

## 1. Statutory Claims Settlement Timelines
- Survey & Assessment Mandate: Surveyor must submit formal report within 30 days of appointment.
- Claim Decision & Settlement: Insurers must settle or repudiate claim within 30 days from the date of receipt of the final survey report and complete documentation.
- Penal Interest on Delay: In cases of unjustified delay beyond statutory 30 days, insurer is liable to pay interest to the claimant at 2% above the bank repo rate from the due date until actual payment date.

## 2. Mandatory Repudiation Protocol
- Every claim denial or repudiation letter must cite specific section clauses and policy conditions.
- Claimant must be provided formal access to the Internal Grievance Redressal Cell (IGRC) and Insurance Ombudsman contacts.
